ABOUT THE EXPERIENCE

A room full of voices.

The project is inspired by the Tower of Babel.

Once upon a time, all people on earth understood each other. But their language was confounded.

This web app is symbolising the meaning of the Tower of Babel. Everyone is represented by a bubble, which pulsates and beeps. Even if you and other visitors speaking the same language, no one would ever understand what you are saying.

Your microphone is used only to shape your sound. No audio is recorded or stored.
